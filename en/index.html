CIVILIAN

We are just civilians.

And maybe that is enough.

Wherever you live, whatever you believe and whichever country you love: you want your children to grow up without fear. The people on the other side want that too.

Nobody has to raise a flag against others or look for an enemy for that. We only have to make ourselves known, as what we all are: civilians. No government and no army can do that for us.

We are civilians. We are the majority of humankind. Without us there is no war, and without us there is no peace.

For everyone

For people of every faith and of none, of every origin, with or without a uniform. You don't have to give up anything you hold sacred, or stop loving your country. Soldiers were civilians and will be again, and their families already are. No party, no money, no enemy. We replace no organisation that already works for peace; we want to lead people to them.

What we believe

  1. I don't want war. And I believe the people on the other side don't want it either.
  2. Every child has the right to grow up without fear of bombs, on every side.
  3. Civilians, on any side, must never be targets of violence.
  4. I stand against no human being. I stand on the side of life.
  5. I open my door, my table and my ear to those on the road.
  6. I take a step, as big or as small as I can, and as safe as I can.
  7. I cannot save the world alone. But together we can do something.

We do not say who is right, and we do not say that everyone is equally to blame. We ask no one not to defend themselves. We only say: the children on both sides share the same fear, and the people without weapons did not want this war.

What you can do

  1. Quietly: put a glass of water in your window. Or carry the word CIVILIAN on a note. Everyone understands it, and nobody can forbid it.
  2. Visibly: write the word by hand in your window or in chalk at your door, today and on 21 September, the International Day of Peace. Only where it is safe.
  3. Show it: take a photo of the word and share it with #IAmACivilian. No face, no location.
  4. Be an open door or walk part of the way: a tea house, a café, a kitchen, a community hall or a house of prayer. A glass of water for those passing by.

Safety first: a word in your window or at your door shows where you live. Where that is dangerous, keep the word only with you, as a note in your pocket. That counts just as much. Photos of the word only, no face, and switch off your camera's location first. Nobody should put themselves in danger for this sign.
